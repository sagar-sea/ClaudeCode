How to play

  • Ten random questions (or however many you choose) drawn fresh each run — never repeated within a quiz.
  • Answer options are shuffled every time, so position tells you nothing.
  • Weighted scoring: easy = 1 point, medium = 2, hard = 3. Your raw correct count is shown too.
  • Keyboard: press 1–4 to answer, Enter or → for the next question, arrows to move focus.
  • Everything is drawn from the books. Film-only details are flagged and excluded.
