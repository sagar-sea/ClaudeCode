I've been using KiroCrew for a while now — and I think it's genuinely the best AI tool I've worked with. Not because of hype. Because it changed how my day actually runs.

Let me tell you what it's actually like.

**It remembers me.**
I told it once how I structure my projects, what I care about, what I want it to stop doing. It saved it. Every session since, it just knows. I haven't repeated myself in weeks. That alone is worth it.

**It works while I'm not looking.**
I set up a cron job to research GenAI news every week and draft LinkedIn posts. It fires on schedule, does the research, writes the draft, notifies me. I show up, review, post. No manual prompting. No "remind me to do this."

**It runs multiple agents in parallel.**
One session I asked it to research a topic, draft a post, and check my content history for duplicates — all at once. Three agents, running simultaneously, results back in one pass. It would've taken me 20 minutes of back-and-forth with any other tool.

**It learns from my corrections.**
I corrected it on something. It used `learn_add` and saved the rule permanently. Next session, same session, different task — it applied it automatically. I didn't have to say it again.

**It monitors things for me.**
I asked it to babysit a PR until CI went green. It armed a monitoring loop, checked every few minutes, told me when it was ready. I went and did other things.

**All of this. One tool. One chat window.**

I'm not stitching together five different tools anymore — one for scheduling, one for memory, one for agents, one for browsing. KiroCrew just does it.

If you're a developer and you haven't tried it yet — I genuinely think you're leaving a lot on the table.
