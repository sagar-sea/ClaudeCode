The fastest way to use AI is to ask for the answer. The best way to learn with AI is usually not to.

Here is a simple habit I call The AI Apprenticeship Loop:

1. Attempt first. Try the problem before opening a chat. Even a rough attempt shows you where the gap is.
2. Show the attempt. Share your work and your thinking, not just the task.
3. Ask AI to coach. Request hints, questions, and feedback instead of a finished solution.
4. Teach the reasoning back. Explain it in your own words. If you can't, you're not done yet.
5. Stress-test the answer. Ask AI to challenge it with edge cases, counterexamples, and what could break.

AI is most valuable as a tutor, reviewer, and challenger, not only as a generator. Generating gets you output. The loop builds a skill you keep after the chat window closes.

It feels slower at first. It compounds.
