# The AI Career Skill Getting More Valuable: Traceability

**Created:** 2026-07-28
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 10:00 PDT
**Format:** LinkedIn Post

---

One thing I’m changing in how I use AI:

I no longer ask only, “Did it save me time?”

I ask: “Can I explain where this came from, what I checked, and where my judgment changed the result?”

That question is becoming a career advantage.

Last week, the European Commission published guidance for the AI Act’s transparency rules, which start applying on 2 August. The rules cover cases such as telling people when they are interacting with AI, marking certain AI-generated content, disclosing deepfakes, and identifying public-interest text published without human review.

This does **not** mean every AI-assisted LinkedIn post needs a label. It does signal a bigger shift: AI output alone is becoming cheap; accountable AI work is not.

For anyone building a career with AI, I’d start practicing three habits:

- Save the sources behind important AI-assisted work.
- State what the model did—and what you personally reviewed or changed.
- Build a small portfolio of decisions, not just prompts or outputs.

The people who stand out won’t be the ones who can generate the most.

They’ll be the ones others can trust to use AI well.
