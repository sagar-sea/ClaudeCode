AI Workflow is the biggest productivity shift. Not a better prompt, but a better workflow.

A workflow makes work explicit. You define the goal, sequence the steps that depend on each other, run independent work in parallel, review the output with a fresh perspective, and build in feedback loops so each pass improves the next.

Why is that more dependable than repeatedly asking for the next step? Step-by-step prompting relies on you remembering the process every time. Context drifts, steps get skipped, and results vary from one session to the next. A workflow captures the process once, so it can be repeated, inspected, and improved.

It also shows where people belong. AI can handle execution, but humans should keep decisions about scope, quality, risk, and trade-offs. Those are judgment calls, so they should be built-in checkpoints, not afterthoughts.
