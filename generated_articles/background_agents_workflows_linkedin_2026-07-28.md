# AI Agents Are Moving from Chat Windows to Background Workers

**Created:** 2026-07-28
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-28 21:05 PDT
**Format:** LinkedIn Post

---

Most of us still use AI like a very smart browser tab.

Ask a question. Copy the answer. Start again.

But a recent update from Google points to a more important shift than another benchmark: AI agents are leaving the chat window.

Google expanded Managed Agents with background execution, remote MCP connections, custom functions, and credential refresh. A task can keep running in an isolated cloud sandbox after you close the interface; your app gets an ID to check progress, stream updates, or reconnect when the work is done.

To me, the career implication is simple:

The valuable AI skill is no longer writing one brilliant prompt. It is designing a job that an AI worker can complete safely.

- Define the outcome, not just the request.
- Give it only the tools and data it needs.
- Set checkpoints for progress and failure.
- Keep human review for actions that matter.

When I learn a new AI tool, I try to describe the workflow before I write the prompt.

That small habit changes AI from a novelty into a teammate.

The people who stand out will not be those who use AI the most. They will be the ones who can make it useful without needing to babysit it.

What repetitive workflow would you trust an AI agent to handle in the background?
