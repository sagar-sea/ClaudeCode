**The Real Issue:**

Agentic AI consumes 1000x more tokens than standard AI. Goldman Sachs forecasts token consumption will hit 120 quadrillion per month by 2030. Even as individual token costs drop 90%, aggregate bills are exploding because consumption is growing faster than prices are falling.

The knee-jerk reaction? Cut usage. Impose limits. Cancel licenses.

**The Smart Move:**

Shift from prompt engineering to **context engineering**.

Prompt engineering assumes you can solve problems by writing better instructions. It's like yelling better directions at someone who doesn't know where they are.

Context engineering is different. It's about architecting systems that deliver the right information at the right time in the right format.

**Why This Matters:**

Traditional prompt engineering only touches 2 of the 7 context layers (system instructions and output format). Context engineering orchestrates all seven: system instructions, user interaction, memory, knowledge retrieval, tool access, metadata, and output format.

The result? A simple system with good context outperforms a sophisticated system working blind.

**The Three Rules (F.I.T.):**

→ **Format:** Clean, structured data beats walls of text  
→ **Intent:** Every piece of context should answer the user's actual question  
→ **Timing:** Fresh data only. Stale context causes hallucinations

**Real Impact:**

Leading AI coding platforms now process 500,000+ files simultaneously. They don't just autocomplete—they understand architecture, coding patterns, and how changes propagate across services.

This is the difference between a tool that guesses what you'll type next and one that understands what you're building.

**The Shift:**

Companies that master context engineering won't just have lower AI bills. They'll have:
- Better institutional memory
- Faster onboarding
- More consistent decision-making
- Tribal knowledge turned into shared intelligence

**Bottom Line:**

The companies winning in 2026 aren't the ones spending the least on tokens. They're the ones extracting the most value per token spent.

Context engineering isn't about AI. It's about information architecture.

And information architecture determines how fast you can learn, adapt, and execute.
