**AWS Built Amazon Bio Discovery to Turn Drug Design Into a Continuous Learning Loop—and it will change how you build AI systems.**

Memorial Sloan Kettering faced a familiar problem: designing antibodies for pediatric cancer takes forever. Traditional methods? Up to a year from concept to lab testing.

Amazon Bio Discovery changed that. Using AI agents to orchestrate multiple models, they designed 300,000 novel antibody molecules. Sent the top 100,000 to the lab. Got results back. What typically takes a year took weeks.

Here's what makes this different—and why it matters for every AI system you build:

**The Lab-in-the-Loop Pattern:**

Design (AI) → Submit to Lab → Test (Physical) → Results Return → Learn (AI) → Repeat

Each cycle, the AI gets smarter. Each experiment improves the next. This isn't just a tool—it's a continuous learning system.

**How it works:**

✅ **40+ Biological Foundation Models** - Specialized AI models trained on vast biological datasets
✅ **AI Agents** - Natural language interface (no coding required) that orchestrates models, selects parameters, evaluates candidates
✅ **Integrated Lab Partners** - Twist Bioscience, Ginkgo Bioworks automatically synthesize and test candidates
✅ **Automatic Feedback Loop** - Lab results route back to the platform, refining models for the next cycle
✅ **Fine-Tuning on Proprietary Data** - Scientists train custom models with their own lab data (just a few clicks, no ML infrastructure)

**Why this matters:**

This is agentic AI in production. Not a chatbot. Not a demo. A real system solving complex scientific problems by:
- Coordinating multiple specialized models
- Making decisions based on research goals
- Learning from real-world outcomes
- Getting smarter with each iteration

**The accessibility angle:**

Scientists don't need to code. They don't need ML infrastructure. They don't need to manage disconnected systems. One application. Natural language. Results.

This is what happens when you combine:
- Specialized foundation models (domain expertise)
- General-purpose AI agents (orchestration)
- Real-world feedback loops (continuous learning)
- Enterprise infrastructure (security, scale, compliance)

**The pattern:**

Foundation Models + AI Agents + Feedback Loops = The future of enterprise AI applications.

Amazon didn't just build a drug discovery tool. They showed the blueprint for how AI systems should work: design → test → learn → repeat.

Early adopters: Memorial Sloan Kettering, Bayer, Broad Institute, Fred Hutch Cancer Center, Voyager Therapeutics.
