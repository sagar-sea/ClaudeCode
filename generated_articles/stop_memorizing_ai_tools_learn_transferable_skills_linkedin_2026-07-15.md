# Stop Memorizing AI Tools. Learn These 5 Transferable Skills Instead

**Created:** 2026-07-15
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-15 (initial save)
**Format:** LinkedIn Post

---

**Stop memorizing AI tools. They'll be obsolete in 6 months.**

Every week there's a new "top 10 AI tools" list. A new framework. A new model to learn.

Here's the uncomfortable truth: the half-life of tool knowledge in AI is now measured in months. OpenCode replaced yesterday's favorite coding agent. GPT-5.6 just replaced GPT-5.5 as the default. The cheatsheet you saved in January is already stale.

But some skills only compound. PwC's 2026 AI Jobs Barometer found workers with AI skills earn a 56% wage premium — and hiring managers say the skills that signal "this person actually builds with AI" are never tool names.

Learn these 5 instead:

1. **Eval literacy** — designing and reasoning about model evaluations. The #1 hiring signal in 2026.
→ Start: Hamel Husain's free Evals FAQ: https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/

2. **Cost modeling** — knowing what an agent loop costs at production scale. Underrated in interviews, critical on the job.
→ Start: https://www.llm-prices.com/ + this token-to-total-spend guide: https://medium.com/@alphaiterations/llm-cost-estimation-guide-from-token-usage-to-total-spend-fba348d62824

3. **Delegation design** — breaking work into pieces an AI can own end-to-end.
→ Start: Anthropic's "Building Effective Agents": https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents

4. **Context assembly** — feeding models the right evidence in the right order, regardless of which model.
→ Start: Anthropic's "Effective Context Engineering for AI Agents": https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents

5. **Verification judgment** — knowing when to trust output and when to check it.
→ Start: the LLM-as-judge section of Hamel's Evals FAQ (same link as #1) — trust calibration is an eval skill in disguise.

Tools change. These transfer.

The engineers thriving right now aren't the ones who know every tool. They're the ones who can pick up any tool in an afternoon — because they mastered what sits underneath.
