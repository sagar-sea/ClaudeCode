# The 7 Free AI Tools Worth Your Weekend

**Created:** 2026-07-10
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-10 00:00
**Format:** LinkedIn Post

---

650,000 GitHub stars. Seven free tools. One weekend.

If you want to actually use AI instead of just paying for it, this is the open-source starter kit. No subscriptions. Nothing leaves your machine unless you want it to.

Here's the whole stack, by what you're trying to do:

**Run a model on your own laptop → Ollama**
One command and you're running Llama, Mistral, or DeepSeek locally. This is where everyone starts.

**Get a ChatGPT-style screen for it → Open WebUI**
Turns that local model into a clean chat interface. Works offline, has document upload built in. Your family could use it.

**Let AI use your web browser → Browser Use**
Give an agent a task — "find me the cheapest flight" — and it clicks through real websites to do it.

**Build a team of AI agents → CrewAI**
Instead of one assistant, you assign roles — researcher, writer, reviewer — and they work together.

**Code with a private assistant → Continue**
A coding helper inside your editor that never sends your code to anyone else's cloud.

**Serve a model to lots of users → vLLM**
When your side project gets real, this handles the traffic without melting.

**Train a model on your own data → Unsloth**
Customizes a model with 2x faster training and up to 80% less memory. Fine-tuning on a normal GPU.

You don't need all seven. Pick the one that matches what you're trying to do this week, and start there.

The best AI tools of 2026 aren't the ones with the biggest marketing budgets. They're the ones you can download tonight.
